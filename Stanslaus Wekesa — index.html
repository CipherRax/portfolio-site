careOS

Clinic and hospital management platform

View on GitHub

A multi-tenant platform for the Kenyan healthcare market that follows the whole patient journey, from registration and triage to lab, pharmacy, inpatient beds and billing. Tenants are isolated with Postgres row-level security, events flow through an outbox, and billing runs on a double-entry ledger with M-Pesa and insurance claims. Role-based workspaces, a patient portal and a hospital finder sit on top.

  • NestJS
  • Fastify
  • PostgreSQL RLS
  • Prisma
  • Redis
  • BullMQ
  • Next.js 15
